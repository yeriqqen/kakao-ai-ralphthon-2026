# Editable fictional rehearsal details

These are optional test inputs chosen with the team's permission. Edit them freely before the demonstration. The app does not import this file, hardcode these answers, or treat these facts as information about a real hospital. The AI must generate its own interview and required questions from the actual customer conversation.

## Customer

Start by sending only:

> I want to go to a hospital.

When the AI asks for relevant details, use the relevant parts of this editable response:

> This is a fictional example. I am 28 and have had a mild itchy rash on my arm for two days, with no urgent symptoms. I am in Mapo, Seoul, and would like to visit this afternoon, preferably around 3:30 pm. I prefer a female doctor. I do not have Korean health insurance, so I want to know the likely self-pay consultation cost and whether I need an appointment.

Customer interface: **English** for the first full rehearsal. Test Russian and Chinese in fresh conversations separately.

Do **not** provide prior-visit status in the interview unless the AI specifically asks. If it remains unknown, the teammate can ask about it during the call. Answer in customer chat:

> This would be my first visit.

If the AI already collected prior-visit status, choose another genuinely unknown detail, such as whether the customer can bring identification. Do not force a duplicate question solely to match a script.

## Fictional business facts — change before use if desired

The teammate answers through the laptop microphone **in Korean**, based on the AI's actual questions. These are optional role-play facts, not app defaults or expected model wording.

| Topic | Optional fictional fact |
| --- | --- |
| Relevant service | The fictional clinic can assess an adult's mild skin complaint. |
| Female doctor | A female doctor is available this afternoon. |
| Time and appointment | 3:30 pm is available, but ask the customer to book before arriving. Do not claim a booking was actually made. |
| Self-pay consultation | An estimated 30,000–50,000 KRW consultation, with tests or treatment potentially extra. |
| Unknown customer detail | Ask whether this is the customer's first visit, only if still unknown. |

Useful Korean wording for that unknown-detail question: **처음 방문하시나요?** (Is this your first visit?)

Respond to the actual generated questions. If a question is outside these placeholders, invent a new explicitly fictional answer as the teammate or say that the information is unavailable. For an unavailable-information test, say you cannot confirm one required fact and verify the customer is asked whether to continue or end incompletely.

At the end, listen to the assistant's Korean readback. Confirm it only if it matches the facts you actually supplied. If it is wrong, correct it and verify the summary changes.
