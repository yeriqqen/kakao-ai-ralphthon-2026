HOW TO PARTICIPATE

You are the business

  1. Keep the customer chat in a separate tab on this laptop.
  2. After the customer authorizes the call, select Accept call and allow the microphone.
  3. Listen until the Korean assistant finishes, then answer in Korean as the fictional business. Speak when the microphone is live. Use only fictional details.
  4. For the chat relay, ask an unknown customer detail early. If prior-visit status is still unknown, ask “처음 방문하시나요?” (Is this your first visit?). The assistant should ask the customer through chat.
  5. The microphone pauses during assistant playback and while checking with the customer. Wait until it is live again; do not invent a customer answer.
